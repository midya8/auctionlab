READ THE BOUNDARY

A small model.
No hidden claims.

What this is

Exact synthetic combinatorial allocation with free disposal, nonnegative monotone private valuations, quasilinear utilities and zero seller reserve. Complete finite allocations, coalition optima, and bundle menus. Python independently replays evidence from disjoint bundles.

What this is not

No actual auction or payments. No learned valuations, budgets, common values, false-name or collusion audit, core-selecting mechanism, scalability benchmark or practical market advice. Browser census replay shares its evidence construction; it is not a second independent mechanism implementation.

Known theory, visible arithmetic

The VCG rule and its core limitations are established results. The default is a developed numerical variation of the classical bundle-versus-singletons pattern, not a new counterexample. These 16 controls are developed teaching fixtures, not held-out evaluation.
